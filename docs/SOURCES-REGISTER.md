# Source register

Current catalog: 10 library reports, 11 recorded paths, 51 narrator identities and 68 source entries. Counts include the Prophet role and recorded source entries; they do not mean 51 independent biography audits or 68 different books. Added profile and glossary references do not expand the 10-report exploration library.

This register copies source metadata from data/catalog.json. Every source's owner and use are preserved in the catalog's fields, edges and knowledge items. Report grading refers to the recorded wording/path and named assessment. Software validation verifies these bindings, not scholarly consensus. Unresolved narrator identity expansions remain explicitly marked; missing assessments stay grey. The Prophet and Companion roles remain green independently of narrator criticism.

## The supplication is the essence of worship

Weak (Darussalam), as displayed on [Sunnah.com, Tirmidhi 3371](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3371). Al-Tirmidhi calls this route gharib and states that he knows it only through Ibn Lahiah. This replaces Nasai 518 in the current ten-report library. No unverified additional route is included.

Selected path: `tirmidhi-3371-chain`.

Al-Tirmidhi → Ali ibn Hujr → al-Walid ibn Muslim → Abdullah ibn Lahiah → Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Jafar → Aban ibn Salih → Anas ibn Malik → the Messenger ﷺ.

Narrator profiles checked on 4 October 2026, using the name links on the hadith page:

- [Ali ibn Hujr al-Sadi](https://sunnah.com/narrator/5757): Sunnah.com and Ibn Hajar describe him as trustworthy and a memorizer; al-Nasai also describes him as reliable and a memorizer.
- [Al-Walid ibn Muslim al-Qurashi](https://sunnah.com/narrator/1807): Sunnah.com summarises him as trustworthy, with tadlis. Ibn Hajar notes frequent tadlis and taswiyah. These qualifications are retained; the green colour does not establish the continuity of every report he transmits.
- [Abdullah ibn Lahiah al-Hadrami](https://sunnah.com/narrator/5033): Sunnah.com summarises him as weak in hadith. Ibn Hajar describes confusion after his books burned and distinguishes the transmissions of Ibn al-Mubarak and Ibn Wahb from him. Weakness is not an accusation of fabrication.
- [Ubayd Allah ibn Abi Jafar al-Misri](https://sunnah.com/narrator/5362): Sunnah.com summarises him as trustworthy. Ibn Hajar describes him as trustworthy, a jurist and devout; Abu Hatim also regards him as trustworthy. The page retains other assessments: al-Dhahabi says he is not strong, and Ahmad reports both softness in his hadith and an acceptable assessment. These differing views are preserved.
- [Aban ibn Salih al-Qurashi](https://sunnah.com/narrator/13): Sunnah.com summarises him as trustworthy, as do Abu Hatim, Yahya ibn Main and Abu Zurah. Ibn Hajar records the scholars’ authentication of him and corrects claims that he was unknown or weak.
- [Anas ibn Malik al-Ansari](https://sunnah.com/narrator/720): An Ansari Companion and servant of the Prophet ﷺ, who lived in Basrah.

Ubayd Allah’s profile was verified in the browser. Aban ibn Salih and the existing Aban ibn Uthman retain different IDs and references. The report’s grading is separate from the assessment of each narrator. English hadith text is copied from the matching page, not independently translated.

## When it is the night of the middle of Sha’ban

Fabricated (Maudu’), attributed to Darussalam on Sunnah.com, Sunan Ibn Majah 1388. This report is not established as a saying of the Prophet ﷺ. The chain is displayed for study; displaying it does not authenticate the report.

[Original Arabic and exact English quotation](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:1388)

Selected path: `ibnmajah-1388-chain`.

Ordered identities: hasan-ali-khallal → abdrazzaq-hammam → ibn-abi-sabra → ibrahim-muhammad-sabra → muawiya-abdullah-jafar → abdullah-jafar → ali-abi-talib → messenger.

“From his father” refers to Abdullah ibn Ja’far. The name link in Ibn Majah 1388 identifies Ibrahim ibn Muhammad with Sunnah.com narrator 11814; its teacher and student match this chain. His recorded assessment is “Truthful, Good Hadith”, with Ibn Hajar’s “saduq” and Ibn Hibban’s inclusion in al-Thiqat. Al-Mizzi’s earlier uncertainty remains documented in the chain note. Ibn Abi Sabrah’s accusation of fabrication is recorded in his biography. The fabricated grade belongs to this report and is separate from the other cards’ colours.

Ordered identities: said-marwan → ahmad-abdullah-yunus → anbasa-abdurrahman → alaq-abi-muslim → aban-uthman → uthman-affan → messenger.

## When Allah wants to destroy a person

Fabricated (Maudu’), attributed to Darussalam on Sunnah.com, Sunan Ibn Majah 4054. This report is not established as a saying of the Prophet ﷺ. The chain is displayed for study; displaying it does not authenticate the report.

[Original Arabic and exact English quotation](https://sunnah.com/ibnmajah:4054)

Selected path: `ibnmajah-4054-chain`.

Ordered identities: muhammad-musaffa → muhammad-harb → said-sinan-himsi → hudayr-kurayb → kathir-murra → abdullah-umar → messenger.

Abu Shajarah and Kathir ibn Murrah are one person’s kunyah and name. Abu al-Zahiriyyah is Hudayr ibn Kurayb. Sa’id ibn Sinan here is Abu Mahdi al-Himsi, and Abdullah ibn Umar is distinct from Abdullah ibn Amr. The report’s grade is attributed to Darussalam, not automatically inferred from colours.
